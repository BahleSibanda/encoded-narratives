On beads, and the women who spoke through them

Before literacy spread through the mission schools of southern Africa, Nguni women communicated through beadwork — colour, pattern and placement carried meaning the way a letter would. This project borrows that idea directly: each door in this site is a bead, each story a message encoded in colour.

Encoded Narratives began as an attempt to hold two things at once — the geometry of Nguni beadwork and the lives of Nguni women whose stories don't often get the room they deserve. Every door you can open here is named for one of those women.

Design references: the negative-space, editorial pacing of monaka design's interactive work, and the door-as-portal, zoom-to-enter interaction from Miu Miu's immersive bags site. The colour language throughout is drawn from documented Nguni beadwork symbolism — red for passion, blue for faithfulness, green for fertility and growth, and brass/gold for status and ornament.
