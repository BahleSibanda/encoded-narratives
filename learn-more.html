On beads, and the women who spoke through them

Nguni beadwork is an encoding system. Colour, pattern and placement carried meaning for those who shared the code, much as a QR code carries a message for a device that knows how to read it. This project borrows that idea: a hut, a door, and a story encoded in colour.
